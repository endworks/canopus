# OpenWeather

OpenWeather's master logo, rendered from the vector original the company
publishes in its brand guidelines, at the three densities a client may draw it
at. Their licence makes attribution obligatory from the free plan up, in the
visible part of the application rather than on a legal page, and names this mark
as the form it takes — so the weather service points at these files and every
client draws the same, correct credit.

Rendered rather than resized: the artwork is 198.43 × 112.51 pt, and the PNG
OpenWeather ships is 600 × 340, which is that at 3×. So these are the same
export at 1×, 2× and 3× rather than an upscale of the raster.

Not modified in any other way, and not to be: the brand rules forbid recolouring
the mark, altering its typeface, changing the symbol's placement or distorting
its aspect. That is why there is no dark variant here and no square one — see
`AttributionLogo`, whose `dark` and `square` are optional for exactly this
reason.
